Florida Tree Service Match is a free referral service, not a contractor. We do not perform tree service or any construction work. With the homeowner’s consent, we pass a request to up to 3 independent local companies named on our form (none are listed yet, so no requests are shared). Participating companies may pay us a fee to advertise on this site or to receive requests. You never pay us, and it does not change what you are quoted. Each contractor is responsible for its own licensing, insurance, pricing and work. You are never obligated to hire anyone.
