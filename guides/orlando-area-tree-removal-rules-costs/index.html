Tree removal rules and costs around Orlando, city by city (2026)

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Tree Service Match

Short answer

Short answer: Orlando-area cities mostly charge small fees but attach replacement rules. Winter Park's residential permit is $35, Oviedo's $46 per tree plus a $25 tech fee, Lake Mary's arbor permit $30, and Sanford charges no fee for single-family arbor permits. Florida's s. 163.045 arborist exemption applies everywhere. Angi's 2026 national average is about $750 (most pay $200 to $2,000).

Winter Park

$35 residential permit; replacement trees must be Florida #1 grade with at least a 3-inch caliper, planted within 30 days or the city bills the inches owed plus $150. Near primary power lines, the city clears a 10-foot radius at $150 per hour, or you can hire a qualified line-clearance contractor.[1]

Oviedo, Sanford and Lake Mary

Oviedo requires a permit for any canopy tree (palms, fruit trees and understory trees exempt): $46 per tree plus a $25 tech fee, a survey, and a City Arborist inspection before removal.

Sanford: no fee for single-family arbor permits; the application asks for the reason, a site sketch and photos, and replacement may be required.

Lake Mary: $30 arbor permit; one tree per existing single-family lot every three years as of right if the tree is not historic and canopy stays above 50%; pruning needs no permit unless more than 25% of the crown is removed.[2][3][4][5][6][7]

Winter Garden and Kissimmee

Winter Garden tree permits need two photos, a site plan and an arborist report for any non-invasive tree over 12 inches DBH; permits issue only under state hazard-tree law or with mitigation. City pickup of the tree costs $200 plus dump fees.

Kissimmee handles a request to remove a tree under its Site Development & Tree Removal permit type (Permitting, 407-518-2379). Check whether your address is inside city limits; many Kissimmee addresses are unincorporated Osceola County.[8][9][10]

What it costs

Angi's 2026 national figures: about $750 on average, most jobs $200 to $2,000; up to 30 ft $150 to $450, 30 to 60 ft $450 to $1,200, 60 to 80 ft $800 to $1,500, over 80 ft $1,000 to $2,000; stump removal about $100 to $150. Add local fees: $35 in Winter Park, $46 + $25 per tree in Oviedo, $30 in Lake Mary.[11][1][3][7]

Frequently asked questions

Which Orlando-area city charges nothing for a home tree permit?

Sanford lists no fee for single-family arbor permits, though replacement may be required.[4][5]

Can I remove a dangerous tree without a permit?

At a single-family home, yes with signed documentation from an ISA-certified arborist or Florida-licensed landscape architect under s. 163.045.[12]
