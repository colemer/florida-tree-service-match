What to look for in a tree service company

How our matching works
Check 1 · Credentials

Ask about arborist credentials

  • Ask whether an ISA-certified arborist will assess the tree
  • At a single-family home, signed risk documentation from an ISA-certified arborist can affect whether a local permit is needed

Frequently asked questions

Is this a tree company?

No. Florida Tree Service Match is a free referral service. With your consent, we can pass your request to up to 3 independent local tree companies named on our form.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Northeast Florida?

Often, depending on the city and the trunk size: 3 inches DBH in St. Augustine, 5 in Fernandina Beach, 6 in Jacksonville Beach. Florida's s. 163.045 removes the permit requirement at a single-family home if you have signed arborist documentation that the tree is an unacceptable risk.[4][5][6][1]

What does tree removal cost?

Angi's 2026 national average is about $750, typically $200 to $2,000.[3]
