Frequently asked questions

Is this a tree company?

No. Florida Tree Service Match is a free referral service. With your consent, we can pass your request to up to 3 independent local tree companies named on our form.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Northeast Florida?

Often, depending on the city and the trunk size: 3 inches DBH in St. Augustine, 5 in Fernandina Beach, 6 in Jacksonville Beach. Florida's s. 163.045 removes the permit requirement at a single-family home if you have signed arborist documentation that the tree is an unacceptable risk.[1][2][3][4]

What does tree removal cost?

Angi's 2026 national average is about $750, typically $200 to $2,000.[5]
