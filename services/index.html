Tree service services

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Tree Service Match

  • Tree Removal: Many Florida cities and counties regulate tree removal, but on a single-family home a local government may not require a permit, fee or mitigation to remove a tree if you have signed onsite documentation from an ISA-certified arborist or Florida-licensed landscape architect that the tree poses an unacceptable risk to persons or property..
  • Tree Trimming & Pruning: Trimming before hurricane season (which starts June 1) reduces limbs that can hit roofs and lines.
  • Storm Tree Cleanup: After a storm, stay away from any tree touching power lines and call your utility or 911.
