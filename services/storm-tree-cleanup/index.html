Storm Tree Cleanup in Florida

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Tree Service Match

Short answer: After a storm, stay away from any tree touching power lines and call your utility or 911. Several Northeast Florida cities relax tree-permit rules for storm-damaged or hazardous trees, but each one words it differently.[1][2][3]

Stay away from any tree touching power lines and call your utility or 911. Don't cut a tree that is under tension or resting on a structure yourself.

What to know

  • Fernandina Beach exempts damaged or hazardous trees during and for one month after a declared disaster.
  • Atlantic Beach's code exempts trees destroyed or severely damaged in an emergency when the administrator finds normal permitting would compromise public safety.
  • Jacksonville Beach exempts trees that pose imminent danger or are weakened by storm.
