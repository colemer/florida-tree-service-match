Tree service in Atlantic Beach & Neptune Beach: what's different here

Atlantic Beach and Neptune Beach both regulate tree removal closely, with mitigation for removed trees. The two cities use different thresholds and paperwork, so the right rule depends on which side of the city line you are on.

  • Atlantic Beach's Chapter 23 requires a tree or vegetation removal permit for regulated trees, with exemptions for trees destroyed in an emergency and for imminent safety hazards confirmed by the administrator.[5][6]
  • Neptune Beach's removal application states a permit is required for trees 8 inches in diameter at breast height and greater.[7][8]

Who issues tree permits in Atlantic Beach & Neptune Beach?

Atlantic Beach Building Division / Neptune Beach Building Department. Phone: Atlantic Beach 904-247-5800; Neptune Beach 904-270-2400. Each city issues its own permits. Atlantic Beach accepts applications online; Neptune Beach's building department is at 116 First St. (permits 904-270-2400, inspections 904-270-2430). Neither is the City of Jacksonville.[9][10]

Tree rules differ by city and county. Florida's s. 163.045 overrides local permits for a risky tree at a single-family home if you hold signed documentation from an ISA-certified arborist or Florida-licensed landscape architect; otherwise ask the office before cutting.

Atlantic Beach & Neptune Beach tree service FAQs

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Atlantic Beach & Neptune Beach?

Atlantic Beach's Chapter 23 requires a tree or vegetation removal permit for regulated trees, with exemptions for trees destroyed in an emergency and for imminent safety hazards confirmed by the administrator.[5][6]

What else applies to tree removal in Atlantic Beach & Neptune Beach?

Neptune Beach's removal application states a permit is required for trees 8 inches in diameter at breast height and greater.[7][8]

What happens after a tree permit is issued in Neptune Beach?

The city's process places a sign in the yard, requires any mitigation payment within 7 days, replacement planting within 30 days, and a final inspection; planted trees must survive three years.[7]

Can I remove a storm-damaged tree in Atlantic Beach without a permit?

Atlantic Beach's code exempts trees destroyed or severely damaged during or right after a hurricane or other emergency when the administrator determines normal permitting would compromise public safety; the person claiming the exemption must prove it.[5]
