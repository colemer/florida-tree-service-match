Flood risk and FEMA maps

Nocatee sits inland of the Intracoastal Waterway and the Guana Tolomato Matanzas reserve, so most homes are not in the direct surge zone; the more common storm water issues are wind-driven rain and heavy rainfall. Lots near preserves and ponds can still be mapped in flood zones, so check the address on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center.[6][2]

Who issues tree permits in Nocatee?

St. Johns County Building Services. Phone: 904-827-6800. Nocatee is unincorporated; the county issues permits. Nocatee neighborhoods also have HOA design review for exterior changes.[5]

Tree rules differ by city and county. Florida's s. 163.045 overrides local permits for a risky tree at a single-family home if you hold signed documentation from an ISA-certified arborist or Florida-licensed landscape architect; otherwise ask the office before cutting.
