Tree service in St. Augustine Beach: what's different here

St. Augustine Beach treats heavy pruning as removal and sends the largest trees to a city board. Oceanfront dunes and marsh-side lots on Anastasia Island add salt and erosion to the mix.

  • St. Augustine Beach requires a Tree Removal Permit before removing a protected tree, and counts severe pruning of 33 percent or more of the canopy as removal.[5]
  • Trees 30 inches DBH or larger need approval from the city's Comprehensive Planning and Zoning Board.[6]

Storm history: trees and wind

Recent storms that brought down trees or flooded this area, from NOAA, NWS and local reports.

  • Hurricane Matthew (October 7, 2016): Surge reached the St. Augustine Beach sea wall early in the morning and water flowed across Anastasia Island about 2.5 feet above ground.[1][2]
  • Hurricane Ian (September 29, 2022): The St. Augustine Beach Fishing Pier recorded a peak storm tide of +7.43 feet NAVD. FDEP rated the developed part of Anastasia Island, including St. Augustine Beach, as major dune and beach erosion (Condition IV).[3]
  • Hurricane Nicole (November 10, 2022): Nicole worsened Ian's erosion; FDEP measured about 1.48 million cubic yards of sand lost above mean high water from St. Augustine to Matanzas Inlet across the two storms.[4]

Flood risk and FEMA maps

Homes here can take water from both sides: ocean overwash on the east and the Matanzas River and marsh on the west. Ocean-side lots may sit in coastal high-hazard flood zones with stricter rebuild rules; confirm the zone for an address on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center before planning repairs.[1][9][10]

Who issues tree permits in St. Augustine Beach?

City of St. Augustine Beach Building & Zoning. Phone: 904-471-8758. The city runs its own building department; applications and plans are submitted to the city, not St. Johns County. Parts of Anastasia Island outside city limits (Butler Beach, Crescent Beach) are permitted by the county.[7][8]

Tree rules differ by city and county. Florida's s. 163.045 overrides local permits for a risky tree at a single-family home if you hold signed documentation from an ISA-certified arborist or Florida-licensed landscape architect; otherwise ask the office before cutting.

St. Augustine Beach tree service FAQs

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in St. Augustine Beach?

St. Augustine Beach requires a Tree Removal Permit before removing a protected tree, and counts severe pruning of 33 percent or more of the canopy as removal.[5]

What else applies to tree removal in St. Augustine Beach?

Trees 30 inches DBH or larger need approval from the city's Comprehensive Planning and Zoning Board.[6]

Is heavy pruning treated as removal in St. Augustine Beach?

Yes. The city's code counts removal of 33 percent or more of the canopy and branches as removal, along with root damage from machinery or grade changes of more than 2 inches in the dripline.[5]

Is a diseased or leaning tree exempt in St. Augustine Beach?

The code lists exceptions for a tree that is diseased, injured, in danger of falling or endangering structures or utilities. Florida's s. 163.045 also applies at single-family homes with arborist documentation.[5][12]
